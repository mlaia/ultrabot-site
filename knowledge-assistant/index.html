Before we start

Good questions. Straight answers.

What is RAG?

Retrieval-augmented generation: the assistant first searches your documents, then writes an answer from the passages it found and cites them. It answers from your content rather than from the model's general knowledge.

Does it work with Hebrew documents and scans?

Yes. Hebrew and mixed-language documents are indexed with Hebrew-aware search, and scanned documents go through OCR first. We test retrieval on your own documents before rollout.

Can it respect our permissions?

Yes. Each answer is built only from documents the user is allowed to see, using your existing access groups.

Can it run without sending data outside?

Yes. It can run in your own AWS account, or on-premises with open models, so documents and questions stay in your environment.

How do you know it works?

We build a test set of real questions with known answers and score every version on it, both for finding the right passage and for answering correctly.
