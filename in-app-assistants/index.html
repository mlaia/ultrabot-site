Before we start

Good questions. Straight answers.

Do we need to change our backend?

Usually very little. The assistant calls your existing API through a thin tool layer; we add endpoints only where an action isn't exposed yet.

Which models do you use?

We choose per feature: Claude, GPT or Gemini, through Amazon Bedrock or directly, or open models when the data or the cost calls for it. The model can be changed later without rebuilding the app.

Can it work on mobile?

Yes, in iOS and Android apps and on the web, by text or voice. Avatars can render in the app or stream as video.

Who owns the code and the data?

You do. It runs in your cloud account, and conversations and logs stay under your policies.
