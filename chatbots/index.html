Insights reports

Learn what your customers ask for.
In one email a week.

A monthly summary comes with every plan: what customers asked about, what they wanted and could not find, and what the assistant could not answer. Daily or weekly reports, with missed demand and recommended actions, are an add-on, priced on request.

Demand you are missing

Products, sizes or services people asked for that you don’t offer. If many ask for a shoe you don’t stock, it may be worth importing.

Gaps in your answers

Questions the assistant could not answer, and what to add to your site or to the assistant.

Recommended actions

A short list for the period: what to stock, what to explain better on the site, when customers are most active.

Conversations are not stored for this. Only topics and products are kept, without names, numbers or other personal details, for a limited time. You switch it on, and your privacy policy covers it.

Connected to your systems

Not only answers.
Actions in your systems.

The assistant connects to the systems you already use, and asks the customer before it acts.

Catalog & stock

Answers on availability, prices and sizes, and suggests the right product: Shopify, WooCommerce, Priority or a spreadsheet.

Bookings

Checks free slots and books, moves or cancels appointments in your booking system or calendar.

Order status

Answers “where is my order?” from your order system, without a person looking it up.

Payments

Sends a secure payment link from your payment provider. The assistant never takes card details.

CRM

Opens a lead or a ticket with a summary of the conversation, in HubSpot, monday or Salesforce.

Read-only first

We start with reading, such as the catalog or order status, and add actions once that works well.

Before we start

Good questions. Straight answers.

Does it work in Hebrew?

Yes. It reads and answers in Hebrew and English, including mixed questions, and replies in the language the visitor uses.

Will it make things up?

It answers from your content and links the source. When the answer isn't in your content, it says so and offers a person. We measure this before launch.

Can it run on WhatsApp?

In early access. Today the bot runs on your website. The WhatsApp bot is open for early access: you keep your own number and keep answering from the WhatsApp Business app, and the bot will answer alongside you. First customers go live after Meta’s approval, and there is no date yet. The bot can already hand a conversation to a person on WhatsApp.

How is this different from a rule-based chatbot?

There are no menus to maintain. It understands free-text questions and answers from your content, while actions such as booking or creating a lead follow rules you set.

What do we need to start?

Your site and key documents, a list of common questions, and access to the system leads should go to.
